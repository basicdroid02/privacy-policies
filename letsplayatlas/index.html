Privacy Policy

letsPlayAtlas · Last updated: August 8, 2026

In short: letsPlayAtlas does not collect, store, or share any of your personal information. There are no accounts and no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains how the letsPlayAtlas app handles your information. By using the app, you agree to what is described on this page.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app never asks you to sign in or share details about yourself. In particular, the app does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or contact details
  • Any login or account information
  • Your location
  • Your photos, contacts, or files

Your Information Stays on Your Device

The app does not save any of your information. Things like your score and the number of players are only used while you are playing and disappear once the game ends or you close the app. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

Playing With Others

When you play with more than one player, everyone takes turns on the same phone or tablet. The app does not connect to other devices and does not share any information about the players.

Advertising and Tracking

The app does not show ads and does not track what you do. There are no hidden tools watching your activity.

Children’s Privacy

The app is safe for all ages. Because it does not collect any information from anyone, there is nothing personal about you or your children being gathered or stored.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this Privacy Policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top. Please check back from time to time.
