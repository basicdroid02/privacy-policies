🩸 Period Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 14, 2026

Overview

Period Tracker does not collect, store, or share any personal data. This app is designed with privacy as a core principle — everything stays on your device.

Data Storage

All data you enter — including period dates and cycle history — is stored exclusively on your device using local storage (Room database). It is never uploaded to any server, cloud service, or third party.

Microphone Permission

The app requests microphone access only to enable the optional voice input feature, which lets you log period dates by speaking. Audio is processed entirely on your device and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere.

No Tracking or Analytics

This app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, and no third-party services that collect data. We do not track how you use the app.

No Account Required

Period Tracker does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information such as your name, email, or phone number.

Children's Privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the new version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
